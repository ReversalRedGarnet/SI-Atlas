## What this licence does and does not cover

SI Atlas records are compiled from public sources such as government lists and
published directories. Each record's `sources` field says where it came from.

- The licence covers our own work: the selection, structure, cleaning,
  verification status and last-checked dates of the records.
- It does **not** claim ownership of the facts' original sources. Where a
  source (for example a ministry roster or a police directory) has its own
  terms, those still apply to that source's material.
- Records are not an official service and are not endorsed by the agencies
  whose public records they draw on.
